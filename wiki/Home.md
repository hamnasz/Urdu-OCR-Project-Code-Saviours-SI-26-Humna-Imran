## At a Glance

This project develops **OCR (Optical Character Recognition) for Urdu text written in Nastaliq script**. The work involves:

- 📊 **Data Collection** — 150+ images from books, newspapers, and synthetic sources
- 🔍 **Image Preprocessing** — Converting raw images into OCR-ready binary formats
- 🧪 **Baseline Testing** — Evaluating Tesseract OCR performance and identifying gaps
- 🤖 **Model Development** — Building a custom OCR model for Nastaliq script

## Why Urdu OCR?

Urdu OCR is uniquely challenging because:
- **Nastaliq Script** is highly cursive, flowing diagonally with position-dependent letter shapes
- **Limited Training Data** compared to Latin-script OCR systems
- **Real-World Impact** — digitizing historical documents, government records, and forms
- **Technical Gap** — existing tools like Tesseract perform poorly on Nastaliq text
